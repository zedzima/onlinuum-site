PPC Campaign Management for a DLNA Media Server

KooRaRoo | Media software | September to November 2013

KooRaRoo wanted more installs of its media server from Google Ads and less paid traffic from people who would never install it. Three months of daily account work turned the search campaign into a steady source of installs without a bigger budget.

STARTING POINT

What the campaign had to fix

The campaign was live, but too much of the budget went to clicks that never became installs.

A niche media server

KooRaRoo is a DLNA server and organizer that streams video, music, and photos to any DLNA-certified device on a home network.

Installs, not just clicks

The goal was to increase the number of product installs and remove untargeted users from the paid traffic.

Click prices vary by region

Clicks cost different amounts in different regions, so the budget had to be split to cover every region that mattered.

WHAT WE DID

How the account was managed

Daily optimization from September 1 to November 30, 2013.

Queries

Search queries were refined, and the keyword list was adjusted to raise CTR and attract better-qualified traffic.

Ads

Unique ads were written for each keyword group and optimized continually.

Tracking

Event tracking codes and goals were set up in Google Analytics, so the campaigns could be optimized on conversion data.

Bids and regions

Bids were managed daily, and search campaigns were split by regional click price to spread the budget evenly across regions.
