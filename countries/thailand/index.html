TEXT

Digital Marketing in Thailand for International Companies

Thailand is South-East Asia's third-largest economy: roughly 69.9 million people, a GDP estimated at 1.5 trillion dollars, and a digital population that spends more time inside messaging apps and marketplaces than on brand websites. About 80% of the market is Thai-speaking and roughly 27% uses English, so an English-only funnel reaches a real but narrow slice of demand.

We are not a local Bangkok agency competing for Thai clients. We are an outsourced, productized agency delivering digital marketing services in Thailand for companies based outside the country that need the market covered without opening an office or hiring a Thai team. If you are a Thai business selling to Thai customers at home, a local shop will serve you better and cheaper. If you are exporting into Thailand, this operating model is built for you.

A Thailand market entry strategy here is a sequence, not an open-ended retainer: demand and keyword research first, then a Thai-language search and content base, then LINE OA and marketplace coverage, then paid amplification once tracking is PDPA-safe. Each step is a fixed scope with a defined deliverable and a price agreed before work starts, so you can stop after any stage. Thai-language execution runs through professional native localization partners rather than an in-house Thai specialist, because we do not have one today.

THAI ADVERTISING MARKET

The Thai Digital Advertising Market

Three things here diverge from the Google-plus-Meta playbook that works in most Western markets, and each one changes what a budget actually buys.

LINE is infrastructure, not one more channel

LINE is the default messenger, the loyalty card and, for many brands, the working CRM. A LINE Official Account collects followers, broadcasts to them, runs rich menus and one-to-one chat, and feeds the LINE Ads Platform for acquisition. Budgeting LINE as a single social line item underprices it, which is also why hiring a social media marketing agency for Thailand on Meta experience alone rarely covers the market.

1

Lazada and Shopee own product discovery

In retail and consumer categories, marketplace search comes before web search: shoppers open Lazada or Shopee, compare listings, flash sales and seller ratings, and never reach a brand site. Listing quality, campaign calendars and in-platform ads are a separate discipline with their own rules, so marketplace visibility is budgeted alongside search and paid social rather than bolted on afterwards.

2

PDPA changes what tracking you can run

Thailand's Personal Data Protection Act requires a lawful basis for collecting personal data, explicit consent for most marketing uses, and extra care when data leaves the country. That reaches consent banners, analytics and pixel setup, remarketing audiences, CRM uploads, lead forms and any work an advertising agency in Thailand runs on customer lists. We plan measurement so campaigns still report honestly when a share of visitors decline tracking.

FAQ

Digital marketing in Thailand: common questions

Can an English-speaking agency run marketing in Thailand?

Yes, for the international side of the work. English stays the working language for strategy, reporting and account management, while Thai-language assets are produced by native Thai linguists. What we are not is a local Bangkok agency competing for Thai clients: this page is for companies headquartered outside Thailand that need the market covered without opening an office. We do not have a Thai specialist on staff today, and we would rather say so than imply otherwise.

What does PDPA compliance mean for marketing in Thailand?

Thailand's Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA) requires a lawful basis for collecting personal data and, for most marketing uses, explicit consent, with additional conditions when data is transferred outside the country. In practice it shapes consent banners, analytics and pixel setup, remarketing audiences, CRM uploads and lead forms. We set up consent-aware measurement so campaigns keep producing usable numbers when a share of visitors decline tracking, instead of discovering the gap at reporting time.

Is Google or LINE more important for marketing in Thailand?

Neither replaces the other. Google dominates web search, so SEO and Google Ads still capture demand that already exists. LINE is where that demand is kept: a LINE Official Account carries messaging, loyalty and repeat purchase, and the LINE Ads Platform reaches people who never open a search box. A Thailand plan that funds only one of the two leaks either acquisition or retention.

How do you sell on Lazada and Shopee in Thailand?

Lazada and Shopee are search engines with a checkout attached. Work starts with listing content built for marketplace search rather than for Google, then category and attribute placement, then campaign calendars tied to the platforms' sale events, then in-platform advertising measured next to your own store rather than instead of it. For most consumer categories, this is where Thai buyers compare options before they ever reach a brand site.

Do I need Thai-language content for SEO in Thailand?

For anything beyond a narrow B2B or expat audience, yes. Around 80% of the market is Thai-speaking, and Thai search behaviour does not translate from English: Thai script is written without spaces between words, which changes tokenisation, keyword research and on-page work. English pages can carry B2B and travel-adjacent demand, but consumer and retail queries need Thai content written by native speakers.

Which Thai cities do you cover?

All of Thailand, with planning weighted to where the demand sits. Bangkok together with Nonthaburi and Samut Prakan carries most of the commercial search volume, followed by Chiang Mai, Phuket, Pattaya and the Eastern Economic Corridor. Digital marketing in Thailand does not require a Bangkok office, and targeting is set city by city rather than applied nationally by default.

How much does digital marketing cost in Thailand, and how long does SEO take?

Cost follows the scope, not the size of a monthly retainer: every scope is quoted at a fixed price before work starts, so a Thailand programme can begin with research and a Thai-language search base and grow from there. On timing, SEO in Thailand behaves like SEO anywhere: early technical and content work shows in three to four months, and competitive commercial queries usually take six to twelve. Google Ads and marketplace ads produce measurable data within weeks, which is why most Thailand programmes run both.

What does a Thailand market entry strategy look like?

Research demand first: what Thai buyers actually search, which marketplaces own your category, and whether LINE matters in your buying cycle. Then build the smallest measurable footprint, usually a Thai-language landing set plus tracking that satisfies PDPA. Then test paid search and marketplace ads to price demand before committing to content and link investment. Launching a large content programme before that test is the most common way to spend a Thailand budget without learning anything.
