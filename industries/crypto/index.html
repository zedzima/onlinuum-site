WHY CRYPTO MARKETING IS DIFFERENT

Four Constraints That Make Crypto Marketing Different

Each of these decides what a crypto acquisition programme can look like before the first campaign is written.

Every major ad platform restricts the category

Google, Meta and X each run their own approval regime for crypto advertisers, and the answer changes per country: a certification that clears the United States does nothing in Germany or Turkey. A crypto advertising agency has to own that paperwork per platform and per market, keep it current as policy shifts, and design campaigns that still work inside the restricted formats that remain.

1

Trust is a deficit you start with

In most verticals trust is earned on top of interest. Here the buyer arrives assuming the project is fraudulent and spends the first two minutes looking for proof. Marketing for crypto projects has to answer custody, audits, team identity and withdrawal history before it says anything about features, or the funnel leaks at the first click.

2

The community is the channel

Telegram, Discord and X are where the product is evaluated, where support happens, and where a bad week becomes a narrative. A crypto programme has to resource them as its primary channel, with an owner, moderation and reporting.

3

Cycles move demand faster than any other vertical

Listings, unlocks, upgrades and market sentiment reprice attention in days. Annual planning does not survive contact with that. The operating model has to be a standing system of always-on search and community, with a launch playbook that can be triggered inside a week.

CRYPTO PR

What a Crypto PR Agency Actually Does

PR is the largest spend line in most crypto budgets, and the least standardised. Three jobs sit under the label, and they are bought, priced and measured differently.

Exchange listings and launch announcements

A listing is a dated event with a fixed news window, and everything around it is logistics: embargo timing, exchange comms teams, regional press, and community messaging that lands in the same hour. Missing the window costs the whole news cycle.

  • Embargo and timing coordination across regions
  • Announcement copy cleared with exchange comms
  • Community and press messaging released together

PR services

Blockchain PR and tier-1 coverage

A blockchain PR agency earns tier-1 attention with a story that stands up outside the category: a regulatory first, real usage numbers, a security disclosure handled well.

  • Founder and researcher positioning
  • Data-led stories built from on-chain evidence
  • Crisis and incident communication prepared in advance

Press release distribution across crypto media

Crypto press release distribution is a real, repeatable channel with its own tiering, pricing and syndication behaviour. Treated as a wire buy it produces nothing. Treated as a distribution plan tied to search and community, it holds a launch together.

  • Outlets tiered by the audience they actually reach
  • Syndication mapped against target-market search
  • Multilingual releases for markets outside English

STILL HAVE QUESTIONS?

Questions about crypto and Web3 marketing

Scope, cost, channels and how the work is measured.

Do you market crypto companies or fintech companies?

Both, on separate pages, because they are separate programmes. This page is for crypto-native products: exchanges, wallets, L1 and L2 chains, DeFi protocols, token issuers, NFT and Web3 apps. Licensed neobanks, payment platforms, insurtech and lending live on our fintech page at industries/fintech/. The split matters because the constraint is different: fintech marketing is shaped by financial disclosure rules, while crypto marketing is shaped by advertising platform policy and a trust deficit. Hybrid products such as a licensed custodian or a stablecoin payment gateway usually belong on the fintech side, and we will say so in the first call.

Can you run ads for a crypto product?

Yes, and the first step is certification. Google, Meta and X each approve crypto advertisers separately, and approval is granted per country, so a live campaign in one market says nothing about the next one. We run and renew your certification per platform and market, write landing pages to pass review the first time, and keep reserve inventory such as Telegram Ads and crypto-native networks ready so a restriction on one platform does not stop acquisition.

Do you do NFT and DeFi marketing?

Yes. NFT work runs on collection, marketplace and mint pages, with NFT SEO and community timing built around the drop calendar. DeFi marketing services centre on explaining a protocol accurately to an audience that will read the docs, plus the trust surface around audits, TVL history and custody. Web3 games and GameFi products fall here too: the thing being marketed is the token economy, so the programme follows the crypto marketing model on this page.

Do you work with exchanges?

Yes. Crypto exchange marketing is the most operationally demanding shape of work on this page, because acquisition, listings and community all run on the same calendar. A listing is a dated event with a fixed news window, so the announcement, the regional press, the paid push and the community messaging have to release together. Alfacash, Alfa Coins and Ethplorer are crypto-native clients of ours, and exchange and market-data products are the shape we know best.

How do you handle Telegram and Discord communities?

As a primary channel with an owner, a calendar and reporting. That means native operators per language and time zone, moderation standards and escalation paths agreed before launch, and community activity reported next to paid and organic numbers. Paid work inside Telegram runs through two fixed scopes, telegram crypto marketing via Telegram Ads and paid placements in vetted channels, so each buy comes with documented pricing and reporting.

Do you run KOL and influencer campaigns?

Yes, and the work is mostly vetting. Crypto KOL marketing goes wrong when placements are booked on follower count, so we screen for real holder overlap, check past campaign behaviour, and agree disclosure wording that will still look acceptable in a year. Crypto influencer marketing then runs on the same calendar as community and paid, and every placement is measured on wallet connects and deposits.

How much does crypto marketing cost?

An audit covering ad-policy status, demand mapping and funnel review starts at a few thousand dollars. Ongoing work is priced as productized scopes based on the channels, markets and languages in play, so you can see what each part costs before committing. PR and KOL placements are pass-through media costs quoted separately from the work, because in this vertical they are frequently the largest line and should never be buried inside a retainer.

How do you measure crypto marketing?

On funded users. We join campaign data to on-chain events so a channel is judged on wallet connects, first deposits and retained balance, with one set of definitions used across every market. That makes budget decisions boring in a good way: the channel that produced funded users keeps the money, and cohorts are followed past the acquisition week so a spike that leaves nothing behind is visible.

Do you work in languages other than English?

Yes. We execute across 17+ languages with native specialists, which in this vertical usually means German, Turkish, Portuguese, Spanish, Polish, French and Russian alongside English. Content, community moderation and paid campaigns are produced natively per market. A translated announcement in a Telegram group reads as a translated announcement and converts like one.
