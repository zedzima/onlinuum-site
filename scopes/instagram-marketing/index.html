WHAT INSTAGRAM MARKETING SOLVES

An Instagram Account Grows on Content People Save and Share

Instagram's recommendations reward posts that people watch, save, share and reply to. The work below is built around earning those signals honestly.

Growth is organic, and we say so in writing

We do not buy followers, likes, views or comments, run follow and unfollow bots, or join engagement pods. These break Instagram's terms, inflate numbers nobody can sell to, and put the account at risk of reduced reach or removal. Growth comes from content, collaborations, community work and paid reach that is bought openly through Meta Ads.

1

Each format has a different job

Reels reach people who do not follow the account yet. Carousels earn saves and explain a product in steps. Stories keep existing followers close and carry link stickers. The grid plan assigns each format a job, so the month's volume goes where it moves the account forward.

2

The inbox is part of the sale

On Instagram, buyers ask about prices, sizes, bookings and delivery in comments and DMs. A reply the next day often loses the sale. Community management runs on an agreed policy and routes the questions it cannot answer to your sales or support team.

3
SUB-SCOPES

What Sits Inside Instagram Marketing

The work splits into three operating areas. They are usually bought together, but when an account stalls it helps to know which one is missing.

Instagram content management

The planning and publishing layer: account role, content pillars, the monthly grid, captions, carousels, Stories and the scheduling of Reels. It decides what gets posted, in which format and in what order, and it drops formats that are not working. Reels themselves are produced in the social content creation scope or by your team.

A

Instagram community management

The response layer: comments, DMs, Story replies, mentions and tags. It works from a written policy for what gets answered, escalated, hidden or reported, with saved replies for the common questions and a monthly log of what people keep asking.

B

Instagram account operations

The maintenance layer: bio, links, Highlights, contact options, Shop and product tags, collaborator access and permission hygiene in Meta Business settings. Most of it is set once and checked monthly, and it is where small errors, such as a broken bio link or an expired Highlight, quietly cost visits.

INSTAGRAM MARKETING FAQ

Tasks, Base Module, Growth, Reels, and Languages

What do your Instagram marketing services include?

Five tasks, bought individually or together: Instagram strategy and grid plan, posts, carousels and Stories publishing, community and DM management, profile, Highlights and Shop setup, and Insights reporting. Setup is one-time; the rest runs monthly. Reels production, design, ads and influencer fees are separate.

What is included in the $1,100 base module?

The standalone base module combines Instagram Strategy & Grid Plan S for one account ($400), Posts, Carousels & Stories Publishing S for up to 12 posts ($400), and Insights Reporting S for one account ($300). Community and DM management and the profile and Shop setup are added separately.

How much does Instagram management cost?

The base module is $1,100 per month for one account. Publishing runs $400, $700 or $1,200 per month for up to 12, 24 or 48 posts, community and DM management runs $300, $600 or $1,000 per month, and the one-time profile and Shop setup is $300 for one account. Prices cover one brand, one language and one primary country. You can configure the exact combination above and see the number before speaking to anyone.

Do you buy followers or use growth bots?

No. We do not buy followers, likes, views or comments, run follow and unfollow automation, or join engagement pods. These break Instagram's terms of use and can lead to reduced reach or account removal. Growth comes from content people save and share, collab posts with creators, community work, and paid reach bought openly through Meta Ads.

Do you make Reels?

Reels production sits in the social content creation scope, which covers short vertical video for Reels, TikTok and Shorts. This scope plans the Reels slots in the grid, writes the captions and publishes them. Reels supplied by your team or the content creation scope count toward the monthly post volume.

Is Instagram advertising included?

No. Instagram ads are bought through Meta Ads Manager and managed in the Meta Ads scope, with a separate ad budget. The two work well together: organic posts that perform can be turned into ads, and the ads send new people to an account that already looks active.

Can you set up Instagram Shop?

Yes, where the account and country are eligible. The profile, Highlights and Shop setup task connects the product catalogue through Meta Commerce Manager, sets up product tagging, and checks the account against Meta's commerce eligibility rules. The catalogue itself, product data and checkout settings come from your side.

How are extra languages and countries priced?

Published prices cover one language and one primary country. Each additional language is added in the order, because a second-language account, or a second-language audience on the same account, needs its own grid, captions, moderation and approvals rather than a translation pass over the first.

Who owns the account and the content?

You do. We work through named, revocable roles in your Meta Business settings, with an access log, and never ask for the account password or move the account into a business portfolio we own. Posts, captions, grid plans, moderation policies and reports stay yours, and the documentation goes with them if you move the work in-house or to another supplier.
