WHAT AN AUDIT AND STRATEGY SOLVES

Decide What Each Network Is For Before Paying to Run It

Many brands keep accounts open because they always have. The audit shows what each account does today, and the strategy decides what it should do from now on.

A busy account can still reach the wrong people

Posting volume and follower counts say little on their own. The audit looks at who follows, who engages, which posts lead to clicks, and how much of the following looks genuine, so the brand knows what it is actually working with.

1

Competitors set the baseline the audience compares you to

Research shows what similar brands publish, how often, and what response they get in each market. The strategy can then aim at the openings competitors leave and avoid copying the category leader.

2

A strategy is only useful if a team can run it

The output names the networks, cadence, formats, owners, inputs and the KPIs each network is judged by. An in-house team, a freelancer or a managed service can pick it up and start the next week.

3
SUB-SCOPES

What Sits Inside Social Media Audit and Strategy

Three pieces of work that are usually bought together. Each can also be ordered on its own when the others already exist.

Social media audit

The diagnostic layer. Each account is reviewed against the same checklist: profile completeness, content and format history, posting consistency, engagement rate and its quality, follower quality, response to comments and messages, and whether social traffic can be traced in analytics. Signs of bought followers, engagement pods or inactive audiences are reported plainly, because a strategy built on inflated numbers sets the wrong targets. The result is a scorecard per network and a prioritised list of fixes.

A

Social media strategy and consulting

The decision layer. Using the audit and the audience and competitor research, the strategy sets the job of each network, the audiences it serves, the content pillars and formats, the cadence the team can sustain, the community rules, and the KPIs. It also says which networks to close or pause. The strategy is written up and walked through with the client's team, and questions during rollout are answered by the same specialist who wrote it.

B

Profile setup and market adaptation

The implementation layer that is cheap to do once and expensive to leave broken. Profiles are set up or corrected so names, bios, links, highlights, categories and contact options match the strategy, with admin roles assigned by name inside accounts the client owns. For each additional market and language, the plan is adapted to the local network mix, tone, posting times and sensitivities, including the decision between a local account and a shared one.

SOCIAL MEDIA AUDIT FAQ

Audit, Strategy, Pricing, and Languages

What does a social media audit include?

Each account in scope is reviewed against the same checklist: profile completeness, content and format history, posting consistency, engagement and follower quality, response to comments and messages, and how social traffic is tracked in analytics. You get a scorecard per network and a prioritised list of fixes. Audience and competitor research, the strategy itself, profile setup and market adaptation are separate tasks that can be added to the same order.

What is included in the $1,000 base module?

The base module combines Social Media Audit S ($400) and Social Media Strategy S ($600) for 1-2 networks, one brand, one language and one primary country. It is a one-time fee. Audience and competitor research, profile setup and extra markets are added separately.

How much do social media audit and strategy services cost?

The base module is $1,000 one-time. Every task scales on its own: the audit runs $400, $700 or $1,000 for 1-2, 3-4 or 5+ networks, and the strategy $600, $1,000 or $1,600 for the same ranges. Every task at its largest tier comes to $5,200. You can configure the exact combination above and see the number before speaking to anyone.

Which networks do you audit?

Any network the brand runs or is considering: usually Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, TikTok, YouTube, X, Telegram, Reddit or Pinterest. Each network is reviewed separately because formats, audiences and metrics differ, and the strategy may recommend closing a network that costs effort and brings nothing back.

Do you check for fake followers or bought engagement?

Yes. The audit looks at follower growth patterns, engagement from inactive or irrelevant accounts, and signs of engagement pods or purchased activity, and reports what it finds plainly. We do not buy followers or engagement for any client, and the strategy sets targets on genuine audience response.

Is this the same as social media consulting?

It is the structured version of it. The strategy task is written by a named social media specialist, walked through with your team, and questions during the 90-day rollout go back to the same person. If you need ongoing advice instead of a one-time plan, that can be scoped as a custom engagement.

Do we have to buy social media management afterwards?

No. The strategy is written so an in-house team, a freelancer or another supplier can run it. If you do want us to run it, it carries straight into social media management, content creation or a platform programme without a second discovery phase.

How are extra languages and markets priced?

Published prices cover one brand, one language and one primary country. Each additional market and language is added in the order through the Market & Language Adaptation task, because a second market often needs a different network mix, tone, posting times and sometimes its own account.

What access do you need, and who owns the accounts?

Read access to the accounts and analytics for the audit, and admin roles only where profile setup is ordered. Access is given to named people, can be revoked at any time, and is logged. The accounts, the audit and the strategy document stay yours.
