Fixed Prices Need Fixed Boundaries

The topic is already chosen

This scope starts after the decision. Which pages to build, in what order, against which clusters, on what publishing rhythm, is a strategy question with its own scope of work. Bring the topic list or the editorial calendar and every piece here has a price; bring an empty page and the first thing to buy is that decision.

SEO content is written to an approved brief

SEO content writing means the target query, the intent type, the heading structure, and the entities to cover are agreed before drafting. That brief comes from the On-Page SEO scope or from your own team. Our writers work inside it. What makes the text SEO friendly is the brief plus the on-page essentials shipped with every piece.

Source access decides whether a long asset is priceable

A blog post can be written from public sources. A case study needs your numbers and one interview. A white paper needs named sources or subject-matter access. Where that access does not exist, we say so before the order, so you never pay a firm price for a thin asset.

HOW A WRITING ORDER RUNS

From Brief to Published-Ready Text

Five steps. One feedback owner. One revision round.

Brief

You bring the approved topic or full brief, the audience, the format, and the reference material. We confirm the acceptance list, the internal-link targets, and the source access the format needs.

  • Approved topic list or content brief
  • Audience, tone samples, and claim boundary
  • Named feedback owner on your side

Research

The writer builds a source list, runs the gap reading inside the agreed topic, and books the subject-matter interview where a case study or white paper requires one.

  • Named source list per piece
  • Subject-matter or client interview where required
  • Outline agreed before drafting on long assets

Draft

A named specialist writes the piece against the brief, with the heading structure, internal links, metadata, and image brief written into the draft.

  • Original draft written by the named writer
  • Internal links, meta title, and meta description
  • Image brief and caption set

Review

A second specialist checks the draft against the acceptance list, then you return consolidated feedback once. That single round is priced into every tier; further rounds are quoted separately.

  • Editorial review against the acceptance list
  • One consolidated feedback pass from you
  • One revision round included in the price

Handoff

You receive the final text in your working format with the metadata, link targets, and image brief attached, ready for your CMS or ours if publishing is part of a wider programme.

  • Final text in your working format
  • Metadata and internal-link targets attached
  • Source list retained for later fact checks

CONTENT WRITING FAQ

Common Questions About Content Writing

Straight answers about scope, pricing, and delivery.

What is included in content writing services here?

Each piece arrives with an approved brief or topic line, an original draft written by a named specialist, a named source list, the heading structure the brief called for, internal links to agreed targets, a meta title and meta description, an image brief, an editorial review, and one consolidated revision round. Content strategy, translation, conversion copy for landing pages, design production beyond the infographic row, and work on text that already exists are separate scopes of work.

How much does content writing cost?

Pricing is per piece, not per word. Blog Post Writing starts at $500 per month for four posts, Pillar Page Creation at $800 per page, Case Study Writing at $500 per case, Product Description Writing at $400 for twenty products, and Infographic Creation at $400 per piece. A white paper starts at $1,500 for ten finished pages and scales to $5,000 at fifty. Published prices cover the English core and one primary country; each additional language adds 60% and each additional country 40% of the item base price, additively against the same subtotal. Research for a new market is priced separately in the cross-market setup scope.

What is the difference between copywriting and content writing?

The goal of the text. Content writing produces editorial pieces whose job is to be read, understood, and trusted: blog posts, pillar pages, case studies, white papers. Copywriting produces text on a conversion surface whose job is to make someone act: a landing page, a sales page, an email sequence, ad copy, interface text. Different unit, different buying moment, different specialist, so each is priced on its own page.

Do you rewrite or refresh posts we already published?

Not on this page. Every task row here prices new editorial text. When a source text already exists, the work is a different scope of work with its own six task rows: refreshing, repurposing, editing, proofreading, and pruning. It is priced at a fraction of writing the same unit from scratch precisely because the source exists, so putting it through a new-writing tier here would overcharge you. Ask us and we will point the request at the right scope.

How do you use AI, and is AI written content bad for SEO?

We use AI for source discovery, clustering, outline pressure-testing, and quality checks. Drafts are written by people, with named sources and claims a reviewer can verify. Unsupervised generated text is a quality problem before it is a ranking problem: it repeats what already ranks, invents citations, and cannot carry your positioning. If you want AI content writing services in the sense of a generated draft at a generated price, we are the wrong vendor. If you want AI used where it genuinely helps and a person accountable for what ships, that is what these tiers buy.

How long does it take to write a blog post?

A standard blog post takes 3-5 business days from approved brief to first draft, plus your feedback window and one revision round. A pillar page takes 7-10 business days, a case study 7-10 days including the interview slot, and a white paper 15-25 days depending on the finished page count and source access. Batches run in parallel, so ten posts do not take ten times as long as one.

Do you write for B2B, SaaS, legal, or technical audiences?

Yes, at the same published prices. Sector work carries no surcharge. It depends on access: a named subject-matter contact, the compliance boundary, and the claims we may and may not make. We write regularly for SaaS, legal, fintech and manufacturing audiences, and for B2B demand generation programmes where the piece has to survive a procurement read as well as a search one.

What is the best first step?

Blog Post Writing at the S tier: $500 per month for four posts. Bring four approved topics and you will see the writer, the brief format, the review standard, and the handoff before committing to anything larger. If the topics are not decided yet, that decision belongs to a content strategy scope and should come first. If you already have approved briefs from an on-page SEO cycle, we can start against those immediately.
